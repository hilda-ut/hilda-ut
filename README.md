# Hi, I'm Hilda 👋 <img src="assets/emoji.png" alt="code with love" height="30" valign="middle">


I'm a full-stack engineer with about 6 years in Ruby, Node, and React. I like working across the whole thing, from the API and the data up to the part people actually click, and I don't really rest until a problem is properly solved.

Most of my recent work has been on a test-builder product, and it has spanned a lot of ground: the templates experience, a rich text editor, the team's observability, and the React migration of the builder. The rich text editor is the piece I led end to end. I own its architecture decision record and built it across the stack, from the validation rules in the Rails backend and the GraphQL and SMART API contract to a couple of NestJS services and the React editor people type into. I also set up much of the observability, including the Datadog monitoring and a trace-propagation approach for following a single request from the browser down to background jobs.

The part I enjoy most is building the tooling that makes everyone around me faster: an agent that triages our tech-debt backlog and opens safe fixes as PRs, a watcher that figures out exactly which packages to rebuild so localhost stops serving stale code, and a workflow that plans a ticket and runs a first-pass code review before a human ever sees it.

Before engineering I taught for 13 years, which is partly why I care so much about the human side of software, the connection people have with an app and how it lifts their quality of life. Right now I'm working on two personal projects, one in the memory space and one in education. I love meeting other people who like building things, so reach out any time, even just to say hi or grab a coffee chat.

Outside work you'll usually find me with family or fermenting something. There is always a jar of kombucha, fire cider, or preserved lemon in the making in our kitchen. And yes, some days I do wonder whether I should just open a fermentation business instead.
